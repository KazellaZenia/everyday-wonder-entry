Built for [make.fun's "Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3) ($2 in USDC).

One sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment strange and new:

> The fridge stopped humming at 3 a.m., and the silence woke me faster than any alarm ever has.

`main.py` writes that sentence to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
